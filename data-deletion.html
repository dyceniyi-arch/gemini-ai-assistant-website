Data Deletion Instructions

Gemini AI Assistant respects your privacy and provides a way to request deletion of your personal information.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to niyidyce@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your WhatsApp phone number with its country code so we can identify the relevant account.

What happens next?

We will verify your request and, where applicable, delete personal information associated with your use of the assistant.

We aim to respond within 30 days. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.
